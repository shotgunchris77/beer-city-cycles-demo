About

Beer City Cycles

Choppers and Dynas. Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

Not a club. Just friends who ride.

Beer City Cycles is a chopper and Dyna brand out of Milwaukee, Wisconsin. We are not a club. We are a group of friends who ride motorcycles, drink beer and put down miles.

It started with a couple of friends and some pop up rides on the south side of Milwaukee. Then came the shirts, the parties and a bike show. Now we throw the Beer City Cycles Bike Show & BBQ at Puddlers Hall every June and the Unofficial Mama Tried After Party every February, and we print merch for the people who show up.

If it has two wheels and you ride it, you are welcome. Come party with us.

Party

A bike show and BBQ every June. The Unofficial Mama Tried After Party every February. Free kegs, good food and a lot of bikes out front.

Drink Beer

This is Milwaukee. The name is not an accident, and neither is the keg.

Put Down Miles

Pop up rides on choppers, Dynas, FXRs and whatever else runs. Ride dates go up on Instagram first.
